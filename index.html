Between Cinema and Lived Experience.

Makis Evangelatos is a filmmaker/director & visual artist with studies in Film Direction, Photography, and Social Theology. His practice moves between documentary and hybrid forms, with a strong interest in questions of gender identity, queer politics and lived experience.

His work spans directing, cinematography, photography, color grading, music video production, and video art.
